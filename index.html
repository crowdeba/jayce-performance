How We Train

Training Rules

A few simple ideas behind every workout on this site — not rules being handed down, just the reasoning so you know why it's built this way.

Pool First

Lifting supports swimming — it should not interfere with swim performance.

No Failure

Do not train to failure. Finish normal strength sets with roughly 2–3 good reps left.

Quality Over Weight

Perfect technique and controlled reps matter more than adding weight.

Don't Chase Soreness

The goal is to get stronger and more explosive, not to be extremely sore.

Explosive = Explosive

For jumps and other power exercises, stop if speed, jump height, or landing quality starts to decline.

50–60 Minutes

The goal is to get quality work done without turning lifting into another long training session.

Listen To Your Body

If swim fatigue is unusually high, communicate with the coach and adjust rather than forcing the workout. That's not weakness — that's smart training.
